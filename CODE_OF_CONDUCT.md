# Code of conduct

gmRunner is a small project that anyone may use and contribute to, whatever their experience,
background or identity. In issues, discussions and pull requests:

- Be kind and patient. Many reports come from players, not programmers; help them help us.
- Disagree about code and behaviour, not about people. Assume good intent.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination, sexual content or personal attacks, and no posting
  others' private information.
- No piracy: don't share, request or link to copies of games, and don't ask how to get them.

The maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, and block people who don't follow
this. To report a problem, use GitHub's "Report content" on the comment.

This follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
